1. Reconcile this plan and parent Stage4 with actual code/tests; preserve repairs.
2. Select the first unfinished stage and implement its smallest coherent change.
3. Use local semantic/source evidence and existing native/eager/application support.
4. Verify identity, old durable meaning, real PostgreSQL lifecycle and measured
   locality/resource costs. Do not mistake absent-config skips for PG evidence.
5. Fold material decisions/results/status into this plan and Goal0.
6. Continue through every signal, return to Goal0 and execute its next child.
   Keep one child active; no nested goals or corrective parent.
7. At an unavoidable session boundary leave last verified result, remaining gap
   and next concrete action. Reopen the owning child for integrated failures.

## Datomic Software

Datomic Pro binaries are licensed under Apache 2.0, available at no cost, and include all features:

- Unlimited peers and/or clients
- [High Availability](https://docs.datomic.com/operation/ha.html) for failover
- Integrated [memcached support](https://docs.datomic.com/operation/caching.html#memcached)
- Supports a variety of [storages](https://docs.datomic.com/operation/storage.html)

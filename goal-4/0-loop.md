# Goal 4 Working Loop

Use `/home/jake/Developer/atomic/goal-4/0-plan.md` as the authoritative
strategy, status, and completion record.

1. Sync only enough of the live repository, retained evidence, current results,
   and worktree to detect changed facts or blockers. Do not inspect Goal 1,
   erase unrelated work, or turn synchronization into a review project.
2. Reassess the strongest known system contradiction, runtime uncertainty, or
   explanatory gap. Choose the highest-value route as new evidence is surfaced,
   while respecting dependencies between stages.
3. Keep one outcome-bearing executable or explanatory boundary active. State
   what observable result would advance, falsify, or bound it.
4. Take the shortest safe actions that materially change that outcome. Prefer
   retained evidence and focused runtime checks over broad comparison or new
   proof infrastructure.
5. Treat Peer/Transactor overlaps as a risk register. Pull a row into active
   investigation only for a relevant surface/ABI mismatch, executable or
   behavioral contradiction, subsystem-contract conflict, or explicit request.
6. Repair confirmed recovery defects generically and retain one focused
   regression. Rerun only the boundaries whose executable inputs or claims
   changed.
7. Confirm important results in proportion to risk: candidate isolation,
   critical behavior, PostgreSQL durability, single-writer ordering, Peer
   agreement, process ownership, and cleanup where applicable.
8. Bank a supported result, record a localized blocker, or bound the residual
   uncertainty before expanding scope. Do not answer uncertainty with
   review-of-review, residual-by-residual compiler work, or a new global
   comparator project.
9. Fold material findings, decisions, evidence locations, stage status, and one
   resumable next action into `0-plan.md`. Keep detailed evidence in repository
   reports rather than growing the plan into an activity log.
10. Continue until the recovered system satisfies the strong-coherence goal;
    report remaining uncertainty honestly rather than requiring universal proof
    or claiming certainty that was not observed.

If the next state-changing action is already known, take it. Do not substitute a
new plan, tracker, audit, handoff, summary, or proof framework for that action.

Completion language must match the Goal 4 boundary: an unexercised dormant path
is residual risk, not automatically a defect; a green critical path is strong
evidence, not universal equivalence; and a known contradiction cannot be hidden
behind architectural plausibility.
